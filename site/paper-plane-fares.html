Paper Plane

Cheap flights out of CPT

Every morning Paper Plane checks fares from Cape Town International to cities around the world and flags the ones that dropped well below their usual price.

Sample fares. These prices are examples. Live prices appear once the daily patrol is connected to a flight-data API.

How the patrol works

  1. Check faresEach morning it asks a flight-data API for the cheapest fares from CPT.
  2. Remember pricesIt saves every fare, so it knows each route's usual price.
  3. Spot dealsA fare 25% or more below the 30-day usual counts as a real deal.
  4. Report backIt updates this page and posts the top five deals.
